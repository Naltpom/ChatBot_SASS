# AI Policy – Chapote CM Assistant

*This is a translation; the French version prevails.*

Version {version}. Draft: text to be approved before launch.

## What the assistant does

If you turn it on, the assistant reads some of your Chapote CM data to answer your questions, for example "give me a summary of my videos this week". It only reads: it does not publish, edit or delete anything.

## What data

Only what you allow, on the accounts you allow:

- your statistics: views, likes, shares, saves, followers;
- your schedule: upcoming, published and failed posts;
- the titles and captions of your videos, only if you check that box.

For accounts shared with you, their owner decides what may be sent. You can see their settings in Settings → AI assistant.

Your accounts are replaced with names such as "TikTok account 1" before being sent to the AI: it does not see your usernames. **Never** sent: your name, your email, your phone number, your date of birth, your social media login credentials, comments.

## Who processes it

The questions and this data go through chapote.fr (same publisher, OVH servers in France), and are then processed by the assistant's AI provider, acting on our behalf: currently **{provider}**. {provider_details} If the provider changes, we will ask for your consent again.

## How long

Conversations about your data are kept for {retention} days, then deleted. If you disconnect an account, they are deleted immediately. You can erase them at any time: Settings → AI assistant → "Erase my conversations".

## Why this is allowed

Only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time, as easily as you gave it: Settings → AI assistant. Refusing or withdrawing your consent changes nothing else in Chapote CM.
